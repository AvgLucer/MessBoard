About Messboard

Messboard is a student-focused campus food feedback platform. Students can share meal photographs, rate food, leave comments and vote on other students' experiences. Our goal is to make campus dining feedback more visible and accessible.

Privacy Policy

Messboard is a hackathon prototype. Reviews, ratings and uploaded photographs may be stored in the project's database. A browser-generated identifier is used to associate votes with a browser. Do not upload sensitive personal information or photographs containing private details. This prototype does not promise anonymity or production-grade data security.

Terms of Use

Users should submit genuine, respectful feedback about their dining experiences. Do not upload illegal, abusive, misleading or inappropriate content. The platform is intended for campus feedback and demonstration purposes.

Hackathon Project

Messboard is an educational project developed as part of a student hackathon. It demonstrates frontend development, Java Spring Boot backend integration, PostgreSQL database management and community-based feedback functionality. It is a prototype and is not officially affiliated with any college, hostel or mess administration unless explicitly stated.

Contact & Feedback

Have suggestions or found an issue? Contact the Messboard development team through the contact information provided by the project creators.
